Capital raised is applied directly to the $350,000 renovation scope — kitchen and bath remodels, systems upgrades, flooring, and exterior improvements. This structure keeps investor capital tied to a defined, visible scope of work with a clear repayment source: proceeds from the sale at projected after-repair value.
