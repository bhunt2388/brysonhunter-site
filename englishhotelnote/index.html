The Asset

Bryson Hunter Real Estate LLC holds a secured promissory note to Midtown Development Group LLC — the entity behind the English Hotel and the Midtown condo-tower project in the Las Vegas Arts District — personally guaranteed by developer Weina Zhang, and secured by a pledge of 22.5% of the membership interest in Fuyi Properties LLC, which holds 66.33% of the English Hotel itself.

Personal Guaranty: Weina Zhang, unconditional and irrevocable, guaranty of payment (not merely collection), jury trial waived, Clark County NV venue.

Pledge Collateral: 22.5% interest in Fuyi Properties LLC, valued at $5,530,090 per the parties' own signed Exhibit A (English Hotel valued at $36M less $11.4M in debt) — collateral already ~3x the note's face plus interest at signing.

Why Now — The Catalyst

  • Per Weina Zhang (call, 8/27/26): the English Hotel has obtained gaming/casino approval. She is described as a founding figure behind the Cosmopolitan Las Vegas.
  • Per Weina: a lending group is providing a $100,000,000 construction/gaming buildout loan to complete the project.
  • Per Weina: 11 active EB-5 investor-visa capital applications are in process, plus discussions with a Canadian pension fund for project financing.

If some or all of the above close, the pledged collateral sits behind an asset that could be re-rated from a stalled condo-tower to a licensed, financed gaming property — a material step-change in downside protection and upside, contingent on these financing sources actually materializing.

Supplemental Collateral — South Dakota Land

To further secure the investor's $500,000 first-out position, Bryson is willing to include up to $2,000,000 of collateral from a separate, unrelated asset: the Preservation Tract of Cascade Springs Ranch Subdivision, Fall River County, South Dakota — 241.34 acres, free and clear, no existing liens. A Broker Price Opinion (BPO) dated September 2025 from Tommy Wiles, #1 Properties Ranch & Recreation, values the full tract at $30,000/acre — $7,240,200 total, based on comparable Black Hills-area land sales ranging $2,056–$65,699/acre and the tract's access to Black Hills National Forest and Angostura Reservoir — well in excess of the $2,000,000 carve-out offered here as supplemental security.

Combined with the existing Fuyi Properties pledge ($5,530,090) and the $2,000,000 South Dakota carve-out, total collateral behind the investor's $500,000 comes to approximately $7,530,090 — over 15x coverage on the capital in.

Secondary Asset In Play

Bryson holds a signed contract right to 17 condo units on the 5th floor of the Midtown tower (equity-in-kind, ~$700K–$800K/unit median in prior negotiations) — a closed, executed agreement, not a pending negotiation. This stands as an additional, independent asset behind the Note position and adds further depth to Bryson's overall claim against Midtown beyond the collateral pledge described above.

The Opportunity

Bryson is seeking to convert part of this position to cash now — $500,000 — rather than wait out a slow workout or a fully litigated collection. Structured as a participation or partial assignment of the existing claim, not a sale of the whole position.

  • Investment: $500,000 now, as a first-out senior participation in a defined slice of the $1,018,000–$1,818,000 claim (structure: participation, subject to final counsel documentation).
  • Return: $625,000 back by 12/31/26 — a flat 25% return in roughly four months. Investor is repaid first out of any recovery, in full, before Bryson's remaining ~$1.19M interest in the claim is touched. Bryson's position is fully subordinate to the investor's $625,000.
  • Assignability: Midtown Development Group and Weina Zhang have confirmed Bryson has full discretion to assign, participate, or restructure this Note as he sees fit. This is a direct, relationship-based loan between Bryson and Midtown — terms of the participation can be tightened or loosened by mutual agreement, not constrained by third-party loan covenants.
  • Backing: Weina Zhang and Midtown will sign an acknowledgment of the debt and consent to assignment as part of closing. Wade Wilkins will execute a deed of trust/security agreement pledging up to $2,000,000 of the South Dakota Preservation Tract as supplemental collateral for the investor's position.

Why This Is Defensible

  • Signed Note, Guaranty, and Pledge Agreement already in hand.
  • Written admission of fault from Weina (8/26/26: "I'm very guilty on this") and her own statement that she must "give you what you need for legal action" (5/7/26).
  • Formal demand and acceleration letter already drafted and ready to send.
  • Collateral value of $5.53M was fixed by the parties' own signed Exhibit A — not Bryson's estimate.
  • South Dakota tract backs up to a $2,000,000 supplemental collateral carve-out — free and clear, unencumbered, BPO-verified at $7.24M for the full 241 acres — reducing reliance on any single asset's recovery.

Path to Close

  • Execute Participation Agreement and consent-to-assignment (drafts ready, Midtown has confirmed full flexibility on structure).
  • Execute deed of trust/security agreement on up to $2,000,000 of the South Dakota Preservation Tract in favor of investor as supplemental collateral.
  • Investor wires $500,000 to closing escrow.
  • Weina Zhang / Midtown execute acknowledgment of debt and consent to assignment.
  • Investor repaid $625,000 by 12/31/26, first-out ahead of Bryson's subordinate interest.
