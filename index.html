Approach

Three principles. No exceptions.

Capital allocation, deal selection, and partnership formation are filtered through a simple, non-negotiable framework.

i.

Asymmetric Return Profiles

Every deal is underwritten to deliver outsized upside relative to downside. A minimum 50% annualized return threshold is the floor — not the ceiling. Concentration is preferred to diversification when conviction is earned.

ii.

Operator-Led Conviction

Capital follows operators, not pitch decks. Partnership selection prioritizes proven execution, transactional integrity, and aligned incentives. Investments are structured with downside protections — but staffed with people who don't need them.

iii.

Long-Duration Compounding

Time horizon is the most underused asset class. Positions are structured to compound across multi-year cycles, with patience as a deliberate competitive advantage against shorter-horizon capital.
