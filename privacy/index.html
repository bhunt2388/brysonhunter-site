Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 11, 2026  ·  Bryson Hunter Enterprises LLC

Bryson Hunter Enterprises LLC ("BHE," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use, store, and share it, how long we keep it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to brysonhunter.com and its subpages (the "Site"), to inquiries you submit through the Site, and to email, phone, and text communications with us that result from those inquiries.

1. Who We Are

BHE is a privately held investment platform based in Lehi, Utah. We are the controller of personal information collected through the Site. Contact details are in Section 15.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you submit an inquiry, capital application, or referral form, or contact us directly, we collect:

  • Identifiers and contact information: first and last name, email address, and phone number (if you provide it or contact us by phone or text).
  • Inquiry details: inquiry type, capital range or deal size, and the message, background, or deal narrative you choose to write.
  • Communications: the content of emails, calls, text messages, and chat messages you send us, and our replies.
  • SMS consent records: when you opt in to text messages, the date, time, and form through which you consented.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, our hosting provider and embedded services may receive your IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, and timestamps. If you use the chat widget on the Site, the chat provider receives the messages you type and related technical data.

Information we do not seek. We do not ask for Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, government ID numbers, or payment card details through the Site. Please do not include them in a form or chat message. We do not knowingly collect precise geolocation or biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry, evaluate potential investment, lending, partnership, or referral relationships, and follow up with you.
  • To send text messages and emails you have asked for or consented to (see Section 7).
  • To operate, secure, and maintain the Site, detect spam and abuse, and troubleshoot problems.
  • To understand how the Site is used and improve it.
  • To keep business records and comply with legal, regulatory, tax, and accounting obligations, including anti-fraud and know-your-customer obligations that may apply if a transaction proceeds.
  • To establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Legal Bases (EEA, UK, and Similar Jurisdictions)

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we process personal information based on: your consent (for example, text message marketing and non-essential cookies, which you may withdraw at any time); our legitimate interests in responding to inquiries, running and securing the Site, and developing our business, balanced against your rights; steps taken at your request before entering into a relationship; and compliance with legal obligations.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose information only as follows:

  • Service providers. Vendors that process data on our behalf under confidentiality and data-protection obligations. Currently these include: Vercel (Site hosting and delivery); Formspree (processing and delivering inquiry form submissions to us); LeadConnector / GoHighLevel (CRM, chat widget, email and text messaging); Google Fonts (font delivery); and email and telephony carriers used to communicate with you. Vendors may change over time, and we will keep this list current.
  • Professional advisers. Attorneys, accountants, and other advisers bound by duties of confidentiality.
  • Deal participants. If you ask us to introduce you to, or evaluate a transaction with, a lender, investor, or counterparty, we share only what is needed for that purpose and, where practical, with your knowledge.
  • Legal and safety. When required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of BHE, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to equivalent privacy protections.
  • With your direction. Any other disclosure you request or authorize.

No mobile information (including phone numbers and SMS opt-in data) is shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Site does not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies of its own. Our chat provider and hosting provider may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies that are necessary to deliver the chat widget, keep it working across pages, and secure the Site. Some internal pages use session storage in your browser to remember that you have unlocked them for the current tab only. You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings; the chat widget and some features may not work as a result. The Site does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, but we honor the Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out of sale or sharing, even though we do not sell or share personal information.

7. SMS and Email Communications

You can opt in to text messages only by checking the optional SMS consent box on a Site form or on our opt-in page at brysonhunter.com/sms. If you do, you consent to receive texts from Bryson Hunter Enterprises at the number you provide, including follow-ups about your inquiry. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or investment. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

  • Opt out: Reply STOP to any message at any time. You will receive one confirmation and no further texts.
  • Help: Reply HELP or email bryson@brysonhunter.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Our SMS program is for individuals 18 or older.

Emails we send for business development include an unsubscribe option or a way to reply asking us to stop, and we honor requests promptly and within the time required by law.

8. Data Storage and International Transfers

Information is stored on systems operated by our service providers, primarily in the United States. If you access the Site from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, which may have different data-protection laws than your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate transfer safeguards such as standard contractual clauses offered by our providers.

9. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy:

  • Inquiry and contact records that do not lead to a business relationship: generally up to 3 years from our last interaction, then deleted or de-identified.
  • Records tied to a transaction, investment, loan, or contract: for the life of the relationship plus the period required for tax, accounting, and legal purposes (generally up to 7 years).
  • SMS consent and opt-out records: up to 5 years, so we can demonstrate compliance and honor opt-outs.
  • Server and security logs: generally 30 to 90 days, unless needed to investigate an incident.

We may keep information longer when required by law or needed to resolve disputes or enforce our agreements.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including encrypted (HTTPS) transmission, access limited to people who need it, multi-factor or credential-based access controls on our business systems, and vendor selection that considers security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and regulators as required by applicable law.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know and access the personal information we hold about you and receive a portable copy.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising or profiling. We do not engage in these activities.
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not use it beyond what is needed to respond to you.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.
  • Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority or state attorney general.

To exercise a right, email bryson@brysonhunter.com with the subject line "Privacy Request," or call the number below. We will verify your identity by matching details you give us to the information we hold, and we may ask for more detail if needed. We respond within 45 days (or sooner where the law requires), and may extend once by another 45 days with notice. You may use an authorized agent, and we may require proof of authorization. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our response with the word "Appeal," and we will respond within 60 days.
